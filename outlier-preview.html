Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about freelancing on Outlier.

Outlier is an expert contributor platform operated by Scale AI. It connects subject-matter experts with leading AI labs (such as OpenAI, Meta, Google DeepMind, and Anthropic) to provide Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), safety red-teaming, and model calibration.
Outlier processes payouts weekly every Tuesday via PayPal, Direct Deposit (ACH), or AirTM. Earnings are calculated based on approved active task hours and project complexity.
There are no minimum hours required. You operate as an independent freelance contractor. You can work 5 hours a week as a side gig or up to 40 hours a week depending on project availability.
